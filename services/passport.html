Required Documents

Application ke liye zaroori documents ki list dekhein.

Indian Passport ek zaroori identity aur travel document hai jo videsh yatra ke liye zaroori hota hai. Passport Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) ke "Passport Seva" official portal se online apply kiya ja sakta hai.

📋 Zaroori Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Address Proof (Electricity Bill / Bank Statement / Voter ID)
  • Date of Birth Proof (Birth Certificate / 10th Marksheet)
  • Passport size Photo
  • Mobile Number aur Email ID (registration ke liye)

📝 Online Apply Karne Ka Process

  1. Official Passport Seva website kholein: passportindia.gov.in
  2. Naya account register karein (agar pehle se account nahi hai) apne email ID se.
  3. Login karke "Apply for Fresh Passport/Reissue of Passport" par click karein.
  4. Application form mein apni personal details, address, aur family details bharen.
  5. Form submit karne ke baad "Pay and Schedule Appointment" option se fees pay karein aur apne nazdiki Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) mein appointment book karein.
  6. Appointment slip print karke, saare original documents ke saath tay tareekh par PSK jaayein.
  7. PSK mein verification aur photo/biometric process hoga, uske baad application police verification ke liye bheji jaati hai.
  8. Police verification complete hone ke baad passport aapke diye gaye address par post se bhej diya jaata hai.

🔍 Status Kaise Check Karein

Apply karne ke baad, passportindia.gov.in par login karke "Track Application Status" option se apni application, police verification, aur dispatch ka status track kar sakte hain.

⚠️ Important Baatein

  • Application submit karne se pehle saare documents ki self-attested photocopy taiyar rakhein.
  • Appointment date par saare original documents saath le jaana zaroori hai.
  • Koi bhi agent ya third-party site agar extra paise maange "guaranteed fast passport" ke naam par, to sambhal kar rahein.
