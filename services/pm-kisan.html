Required Documents

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana ek Central Government scheme hai jisme eligible farmers ko har saal ₹6,000 teen equal installments mein direct bank account (DBT) mein transfer kiya jaata hai. Ye registration pmkisan.gov.in official portal se online kiya ja sakta hai — bilkul free.

📋 Zaroori Documents

  • Aadhaar Card (mobile number se linked)
  • Bank Account details (Aadhaar se linked hona zaroori hai)
  • Land Ownership Documents (Khatiyan / Land Record)
  • Mobile Number

📝 Online Registration Ka Process

  1. Official website kholein: pmkisan.gov.in
  2. Homepage par "New Farmer Registration" option par click karein.
  3. Apna Aadhaar Number aur state select karke aage badhein.
  4. Personal details, bank account details aur land record details bharen.
  5. Zaroori documents upload karein aur form submit kar dein.
  6. Verification ke baad, agar sab details sahi hain, to aapko har installment automatically bank account mein milne lagegi.

🔍 Status Kaise Check Karein

pmkisan.gov.in par jaakar "Beneficiary Status" option mein apna Aadhaar Number, Mobile Number, ya Account Number daal kar apna registration status aur installment history check kar sakte hain.

🆔 e-KYC Kyun Zaroori Hai

Government ne PM Kisan ke saare beneficiaries ke liye e-KYC compulsory kar diya hai. Bina e-KYC ke installment ruk sakti hai. Ye OTP-based e-KYC portal par khud se kar sakte hain, ya CSC (Common Service Centre) jaakar biometric e-KYC bhi karwa sakte hain.

Ye website sirf jaankari deti hai — apna registration yahin official portal par karein

⚠️ Important Baatein

  • Ye scheme 100% free hai — koi bhi agent registration ke naam par paise maange to sambhal kar rahein.
  • Apna Aadhaar aur Bank Account details bilkul sahi bharein, galti se installment atak sakti hai.
  • Apni e-KYC time par complete karte rahein taaki installment na roke.
