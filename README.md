# Bihar Online Services Help Portal

Ye ek **informational website** hai jo Bihar ke residents ko alag-alag government services ke baare mein aasan bhasha mein step-by-step jaankari deti hai — jaise:

- Ration Card Search aur Download
- Ration Card mein Naya Member Add karna
- Ration Card se Member Delete karna
- e-KYC (Ration Card)
- Ayushman Card
- PAN Card
- Voter ID
- Income Certificate
- Caste Certificate
- Residential Certificate

## Disclaimer

Ye website **Bihar Sarkar ki official website nahi hai**. Ye ek independent informational/guide portal hai jiska maksad logo ko sarkari services ke process ko aasani se samajhne mein madad karna hai.

Sabhi official kaam (apply karna, status check karna, download karna) ke liye respective **official government website** ka link har service page par diya gaya hai. Kripya apna kaam sirf official website par hi karein.
